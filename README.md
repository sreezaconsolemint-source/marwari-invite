# Sunehri Rajwada — live wedding invitation

A self-contained, editable wedding invitation inspired by the Memoria Link “Royal Rajwada” rhythm (opening cover, palace hero, golden-foil date, celebrations, gallery and blessings).

- **Invitation:** [index.html](index.html)
- **Editor:** [edit.html](edit.html) — fill in names, dates and wording; get a shareable link (`#data=`). Empty optional fields are hidden. Drafts are saved in the browser.

## Fonts
Cinzel, Cormorant Garamond, Raleway and Noto Serif Devanagari are bundled under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (Google Fonts).

## Artwork
All paintings, the Ganesha emblem and photographs are original. The reference invitation was studied for layout, typography and interaction only. Their images, video, audio and wording are not used.

Sample couple: Avni Kothari & Veer Rathore, Jaipur, 13 February 2027.
